About

International experience and perspective.

I'm a commercially minded marketer with over 20 years of experience across small and large private enterprises, full-service creative agencies and cutting-edge technology start-ups.

I've worked in the USA, Europe, the Middle East and Australasia, with clients ranging from luxury hospitality and entertainment to professional services, healthcare, and high-tech hardware and software.

With experience in both B2C and B2B, I'm an end-to-end marketer with core skills in:

  • Branding and positioning
  • Omnichannel campaign strategy
  • Content production and distribution
  • Growth marketing and B2B demand generation
  • Search marketing and advertising
  • Community and partnership development

I'm also proud to be part of the strategic communications team at KickDrum Partners, a leading fractional marketing and business development consultancy for technology businesses.

Why work with me

Back to basics, then scale what works.

I get the best results with partners who are prepared to invest in the fundamentals first: truly understanding your audience, honing your message and optimising deliverability.

I embrace technology and all the data and efficiency it brings. But the latest gimmick will never make up for poor planning, vague goals or a lack of understanding. Sound communication principles, layered with the right technology and a spark of creativity, remain the bedrock of authentic persuasion and growth.

Trends will come and go. Technology will continue to enthral us. Authentic connection will prevail.
